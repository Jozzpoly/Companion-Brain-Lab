## 3. q, radial PACE and G4 remain different evidence structures

A1.2t/u/v/w established that these axes must not be collapsed:

### q — relationship quality

Unary semantic evidence about the absolute terminal relationship state relative to the explicit objective.

### radial PACE — relationship progress

Unary moving-frame evidence about how radial relationship error changes through the rehearsed future.

PACE can discriminate where q is effectively flat, and q/PACE can also disagree directionally.

### G4 — cooperation/right-of-way relation

Pairwise relation between concrete commands under one exact player future. It can reject avoidable reciprocal pressure/contact even when unary axes prefer the other command.

G4 is not a unary `cooperationScore`.

A1.2x showed that, in its bounded matrix, G4-undominated and q/PACE-Pareto reductions produced the same final structured frontier regardless of the two tested composition orders. That is useful scoped evidence, **not** proof of a globally commutative selector algebra.
