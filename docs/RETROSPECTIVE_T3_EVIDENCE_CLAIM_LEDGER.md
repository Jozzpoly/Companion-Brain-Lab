## 1. Evidence classes used in this audit

Keep five distinct levels:

1. **contract evidence** — types, invariants, deterministic/unit tests;
2. **controlled scenario evidence** — headless or browser scenario with authored inputs;
3. **runtime/browser evidence** — real integrated application behavior;
4. **participant-first evidence** — behavior interpreted before causal/debug explanation;
5. **Owner experiential evidence** — unscripted feel, trust, readability, annoyance, usefulness, desire to continue.

A higher class does not automatically invalidate a lower one.

It can invalidate a **broader interpretation** that lower evidence never earned.

## 2. S0 — model case of correct scope

Historical label:

**FULL PASS FOR RESEARCH APPARATUS / NOT A MOVEMENT-QUALITY PASS**

Evidence:

- physical/domain contracts;
- real browser recording;
- bounded contact/doorway/wall pressure;
- Owner explicitly judged presentation/movement raw while collision appeared usable enough.

What survived:

- World/Rapier physical substrate was good enough to expose later AI/movement failures.

What was correctly not claimed:

- good character control;
- good contact feel;
- good companion movement;
- final body semantics.

Retrospective assessment:

**EXEMPLARY SCOPE DISCIPLINE.**

S0 is important because it shows the project already knew how to distinguish:

> “good enough instrument” from “good product”.

That standard must be reused now.

## 4. S3 — bounded locomotion competence, not teammate competence

Historical label:

**MECHANICAL PASS · OWNER BROWSER GATE OPEN**

Evidence:

- 49/49 tests;
- long World+physics pillar and doorway trials;
- route/local locomotion integration.

Valid claim:

- the companion gained real static-route/local-motion competence.

Non-claim already stated in the document:

- finished movement architecture;
- good relationship objective;
- dynamic cooperation/right-of-way;
- multi-companion;
- combat;
- natural feel.

Later evidence:

- S3-derived local movement survived into the Foundation;
- Owner evidence showed responsiveness improvement;
- later pathologies came substantially from upstream relationship semantics, temporal realization and robustness seams.

Retrospective assessment:

**DURABLE BOUNDED PASS.**

Risk introduced here:

The local candidate scorer became a long-lived authority even though its weights, 24-direction lattice, 0.55 s horizon and fixed experimental speed were explicitly provisional.

This is not yet a proven mistake, but it is **temporary-mechanism ossification risk**.

## 5. S4 — mechanical naturalness pass later exposed robustness/semantic ambiguity

Historical label:

**MECHANICAL PASS / OWNER BROWSER FEEL GATE OPEN**

Evidence:

- 59/59 tests;
- bounded acceleration/braking/jerk;
- pillar/doorway competence preserved;
- refinement safety checked.

Valid claim:

- temporal shaping can produce continuous physical realization without losing those bounded traversal tests.

Later Owner/runtime evidence:

- autonomous `UNREACHABLE/HOLD/zero-motion` failure existed;
- R1 had to re-open robustness;

Retrospective assessment:

**MECHANISM VALUE SURVIVES; PRODUCT INTERPRETATION NARROWED.**

The project was right to keep S4’s feel gate open.

Critical lesson:

> smoothness is a rendering/embodiment property of a decision, not evidence that the decision itself is coherent.

## 6. R1 — strongest historical warning against “many tests = ready”

R1-4 at one point had:

- 21/21 test files;
- 108/108 tests;
- TypeScript/build PASS.

Additional claim-vs-code auditing had already found and repaired several missing regressions.

Then Owner browser play reproduced an ordinary-state whole-workbench freeze / autonomous failure.

The R1 ledger therefore explicitly downgraded the previous mechanical PASS to historical evidence.

Retrospective assessment:

**CRITICAL PROCESS LESSON.**

R1 demonstrates:

> even a deliberately adversarial and large test suite can remain deeply under-complete relative to unscripted interaction.

This matters directly before OS-PREP-5.

The relevant question is not:

> “did we finally accumulate enough green checks?”

It is:

> “what kind of unknown can only emerge when the Owner interacts freely with this organism, and is the organism broad enough that this interaction is worth the Owner’s attention?”

## 8. CCC — valuable Owner signal with correct attribution reversal

Owner observation:

> first weak spontaneous feeling that the companion was beginning to feel alive.

Tempting interpretation:

> CCC shadow cognition caused aliveness.

Lineage and zero-authority proof falsified that interpretation.

Corrected conclusion:

> the existing movement stack already contained the perceptual signal.

CCC then investigated candidate causes and false-life mechanisms.

Retrospective assessment:

**HIGH-VALUE SCIENTIFIC TURN.**

This is one of the most important project moments because it separated:

- phenomenology: “something feels alive”;
- mechanism: not yet known;
- causal authority: CCC did not cause it.

The unresolved issue remains:

> how much of that aliveness survives after removing stale targets, lexical biases and other pseudo-intent mechanisms?

That question has not been fully answered.

## 9. A0 / A1 / A1.2 — excellent local epistemics, dangerous global optics

A1 accumulated a large number of qualified findings and regressions.

The project repeatedly protected itself from premature authority:

- H1/H2/H3 meanings kept separate;
- q/PACE/G4 kept separate;
- no scalar winner;
- no future voting;
- no tangent pulse;
- no automatic alternate-future veto;
- no singleton=>execute;
- observer non-interference tested;
- World prediction/outcome seams qualified;
- A1 runtime stayed pass-through;
- post-Z4 explicitly said **LIVE MOVEMENT AUTHORITY HOLD**.

This is strong epistemic discipline.

However the cumulative corpus creates a different risk:

> hundreds of successful research contracts can create an intuitive sense that the companion itself has acquired those reasoning capabilities.

It has not.

Ordinary A1 OFF behavior does not use them to choose action.

Retrospective assessment:

**RESEARCH SUBSTRATE: HIGH VALUE.  
PRODUCT CAPABILITY CREDIT: NEAR ZERO UNTIL AUTHORITY IS EARNED.**

This distinction must be made explicit in every future roadmap.

## 10. P2 — intervention competence is not autonomous competence

P2 established a valuable exact seam:

- preview current proposal;
- explicit manual arm;
- one World step;
- factual outcome;
- auto-disarm.

This proves the lab can execute one chosen research proposal causally and inspect the result.

It does not prove:

- the system knows when to intervene;
- which proposal should be chosen;
- repeated policy coherence;
- useful autonomous behavior.

Retrospective assessment:

**EXPERIMENTAL ACTUATOR PASS, NOT BRAIN PASS.**

## 11. OS-PREP — major methodological improvement

OS-PREP corrected a real project drift by moving from internal decision structure back toward player-observable phenomena.

High-value features:

- participant-first interpretation;
- causal reveal later;
- Twin non-interference;
- explicit product questions;
- refusal to invent mechanisms merely to pass a dossier;
- reuse of evidence instead of endlessly adding harnesses.

Material findings include both successes and pathologies.

This is a strong correction.

## 12. OS-PREP evidence limitations

Despite the methodological improvement, most OS-PREP participant evidence is still:

- authored;
- movement-only;
- short/bounded;
- based on four tiny arenas;
- often automated keyboard stories;
- sampled participant frames rather than extended human perception;
- interpreted by Browser GPT, not the Owner.

Examples:

- PH-03 proves a slow-walk baseline can be calm in its exact story;
- PH-04 proves no special right-of-way system is needed in its exact open-space stories;
- PH-05 proves useful doorway separation/reacquisition in its exact bounded episode;
- PH-02 proves stale policy can materially worsen one post-expiry contact state;
- PH-07 proves router lexical symmetry bias.

These findings are real.

They do not establish:

- broad unscripted robustness of behavior quality;
- teammate trust;
- meaningful initiative;
- general constrained-space social understanding;
- a minimum useful partner.

## 15. Recurrent historical pattern

Across S1, S4, R1 and now the pre-OS-PREP-5 boundary, the project repeatedly encounters the same epistemic structure:

```text
internal mechanism becomes increasingly well qualified
        ↓
confidence grows around the exercised dimension
        ↓
a broader Owner/runtime interaction reveals a missing dimension
        ↓
previous evidence remains true
but the implied readiness boundary shrinks
```

This is not project failure.

It is precisely why the laboratory exists.

The mistake would be failing to recognize the pattern now.

## 16. Evidence that should receive the most trust

### High trust

- World/Rapier factual authority;
- hard-vs-comfort separation;
- whole-body traversal/egress semantics;
- failure containment;
- participant/research non-interference;
- source/provenance identity;
- explicit fact that A1/CCC are zero/pass-through in ordinary runtime;
- exact bounded PH findings within their scenarios.

### Medium trust

- NATURAL temporal embodiment as a generally useful principle;
- local omnidirectional spatial selection as a useful locomotion primitive;
- progress/recovery classifications as useful diagnostic semantics;
- history/multiple-timescale behavior as an aliveness contributor.

These remain architecture candidates, not final truths.

### Low trust if generalized beyond evidence

- exact 8-slot relationship semantics;
- exact spatial score weights;
- current fixed 3 m/s capability assumption;
- current expiry duration;
- lexical route/slot tie handling;
- shadow PACE as gameplay pace;
- “no right-of-way mechanism needed” outside tested open-space cases;
- “doorway cooperation solved” beyond the specific authored doorway;
- “participant-readable” as a broad human judgement.

## 17. T3 conclusion

The project’s biggest historical error has **not** been careless testing.

Testing discipline is unusually strong.

The recurring risk is:

> **correct narrow evidence gradually acquiring broader intuitive meaning because the surrounding apparatus becomes sophisticated.**

Therefore the pre-Owner-Sandbox gate must be phrased in product terms, not evidence-volume terms.

The next review tracks must answer:

1. what genuine live teammate function is missing?
2. whether the narrow movement organism is still a high-information Owner stimulus;
3. whether one small pre-Sandbox addition would expose a much more meaningful product question without starting another architecture campaign.

Until that is answered, green #1358 or a perfect pinned deployment cannot authorize OS-PREP-5.
