## 1. Why OS-PREP-5 changed

The pre-Sandbox retrospective rejected the previous broad question:

> “Does the accumulated Companion system feel like a competent/readable teammate?”

The current organism cannot honestly answer that question.

Its live authority is still primarily an embodied player-relative movement relationship. It has not yet implemented the original minimum-useful-partner dimensions such as threat responsibility, assistance, engagement/disengagement, readiness, commands or tactical focus.

The revised Owner question is therefore:

> **Is the current embodied movement/relationship substrate good enough — and sufficiently worth preserving — that Companion Brain Lab should stop treating locomotion as its main research frontier and begin the first true minimum-useful-partner experiment?**

This stage prepares that question and nothing broader.

## 7. What the Owner run is allowed to answer

The Owner judgement target is deliberately narrow:

- does the companion have useful physical presence?
- is movement responsive enough?
- is motion natural enough?
- is spacing acceptable?
- does the companion obstruct?
- does temporary separation feel reasonable?
- does reacquisition feel useful?
- are routes/arcs legible?
- do apparent autonomous choices feel useful, fake or arbitrary?
- are any known pathologies actually salient during free play?
- most importantly: **is this movement foundation now good enough that locomotion should stop being the project's main research frontier?**

The first run is not a checklist. These are post-hoc interpretation dimensions.
