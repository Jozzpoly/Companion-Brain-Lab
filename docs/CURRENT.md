## 1. What we are building

The target is not a better follower, pathfinder or behavior list.

The project exists to discover how to build a **competent embodied partner present in the same situation as the player**:

- strong cheap local live cognition;
- useful autonomy without babysitting;
- readable initiative;
- player ↔ companion reactions that feel alive because they have real causes;
- combat and preparation for combat;
- fast player direction later, interpreted as intent rather than rigid puppeteering where appropriate;
- one companion first, later a few;
- higher cognition / LLM later where it adds value rather than replacing the live brain.

Experiential north star:

> During unscripted play, the Owner increasingly treats the companion as a teammate: can rely on it without babysitting, can predict enough to coordinate, notices meaningful initiative, and can correct or direct it quickly when desired.
