# OS-PREP-4 — Participant-First Dry-Run Synthesis

Status: **CLOSED ENOUGH BY EVIDENCE REUSE · OWNER FEEL NOT CLAIMED**
Date: **2026-09-19**

OS-PREP-4 was originally planned as a separate participant-first dry run.

The completed OS-PREP-3 campaign has already executed that protocol repeatedly. A new broad replay would mostly duplicate evidence and increase apparatus cost without resolving a new product uncertainty.

This synthesis therefore closes the Browser-GPT pre-Owner portion by reusing the existing participant artifacts and their later causal reads.

## 1. Gate being tested

OS-PREP-4 asks:

> Can the current behavior be interpreted at the participant surface before research vocabulary explains it?

This is not the final teammate-quality gate.

Only the Owner can judge feel, trust, desirability and whether the companion actually reads as a teammate.

## 2. Cross-phenomenon participant-first evidence

### PH-01 — turns / reversals

Participant surface:

- motion can look like a smooth evolving arc.

Later causal read:

- relationship world targets can relocate abruptly by metres;
- NATURAL temporal realization masks the discontinuity;
- symbolic side identity is not sufficient evidence of spatial intention.

Result:

**readable motion, but smoothness can counterfeit continuous upstream meaning.**

### PH-02 — semantic expiry / external disturbance

Participant surface:

- sustained close interaction and delayed egress are visible without needing A1 labels.

Later causal read:

- canonical semantics are already NONE;
- legacy retained directional policy continues acting;
- same-state directionless alternatives break contact faster and without displacing the player.

Result:

**participant-visible pathology with a concrete stale-policy cause.**

### PH-03 — slow pace

Participant surface:

- companion settles into calm slow tracking beside a ~0.6 m/s player;
- no obvious chase/over-brake oscillation.

Later causal read:

- live baseline converges to the player pace;
- shadow PACE still reports a much faster 1.65 m/s desired speed.

Result:

**the participant surface correctly protects us from promoting a misleading research metric.**

### PH-04 — open-space passing

Participant surface:

- head-on / cross-front episodes look cooperative;
- player continues almost completely unhindered.

Later causal read:

- no true player-flow conflict occurs;
- no explicit right-of-way or yield policy is responsible or required.

Result:

**cooperative-looking behavior without inventing a cooperation FSM.**

### PH-05 — doorway

Participant surface:

- temporary separation;
- autonomous reacquisition;
- settle.

Later causal read:

- player agency remains essentially intact;
- the companion returns through the doorway without recovery authority or special chokepoint policy.

Result:

**a genuinely useful bounded teammate behavior produced by simpler existing mechanisms.**

### PH-08 — continuity

Participant surface:

- doorway/reacquisition reads as one coherent episode.

Later causal read:

- upstream relationship/objective state makes material discrete jumps;
- NATURAL realization spreads those revisions over tens of milliseconds;
- useful reacquisition continues after canonical directional semantics expire.

Result:

**product continuity is real, but it is not proof of one continuous epistemic intention.**

### PH-07 — symmetric pillar

Participant surface:

- both runs traverse without collision;
- isolated behavior can read as an ordinary route choice.

Later paired/causal read:

- relationship geometry mirrors correctly;
- router lexical tie ordering chooses the same north route at t0 in both mirrored cases;
- embodied mirror error later grows to ~0.44 m.

Result:

**a deterministic implementation detail can silently become topology preference even when an isolated run looks plausible.**

## 3. What the dry run establishes

Before Owner time is spent, the current evidence already demonstrates that Browser GPT can distinguish at least four important classes:

1. useful behavior with a simple causal explanation;
2. participant-visible pathology;
3. plausible-looking behavior whose internal explanation is less coherent than its motion;
4. invisible/ambiguous implementation bias exposed only by a discriminating mirror.

That is sufficient for the pre-Owner bias filter.

The causal microscope is providing explanations after behavioral interpretation instead of manufacturing the interpretation first.

## 4. What remains specifically human

Do not promote this synthesis into a teammate-quality PASS.

The unresolved human questions include:

- does the companion feel cooperative rather than merely non-obstructive?
- does temporary separation build or reduce trust?
- are its routes and arcs legible enough during unscripted play?
- which initiative feels useful rather than arbitrary?
- which known pathologies are actually salient?
- does the whole organism feel like the beginning of a teammate?

These require the Owner Sandbox.

## 5. Known material evidence carried into Owner Sandbox

Positive bounded behaviors:

- calm slow-pace tracking;
- open-space passing with preserved agency;
- doorway separation -> reacquisition -> settle.

Known pathologies / risks:

- stale retained semantic policy can produce close-contact pressure;
- deterministic router tie ordering can create unearned topology-side bias;
- downstream NATURAL continuity can mask discrete upstream objective changes.

Known non-authorities:

- A1 remains research/pass-through unless explicitly authorized;
- P2 is not an automatic selector;
- current PACE shadow is not gameplay authority;
- no right-of-way/yield policy has been established.

## 6. PH-09 / P2 decision

Do not force a P2 A/B merely to complete the phenomenon list.

The strongest existing PH-02 pathology is created through the controlled `semanticpush` apparatus, while P2 deliberately rejects simultaneous `semanticpush`.

Reproducing that exact state for P2 would require new state-setting machinery or weakening an intentional guardrail.

The already-qualified tick-zero head-on P2 state is also low-information for product improvement because PH-04 shows that the normal baseline already preserves player agency extremely well there.

Therefore PH-09 remains a deferred experimental capability until natural Owner or automated evidence provides a state where one-step intervention can discriminate a real product hypothesis.
