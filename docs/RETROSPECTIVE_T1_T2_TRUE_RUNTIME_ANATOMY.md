## 1. Executive finding

The current project is **not yet a minimum useful local partner in the original sense**.

It is much more accurately described as:

> a highly instrumented, physically grounded, robust and temporally embodied **player-relative locomotion/cooperation substrate**, with a shallow live relationship objective and a very large zero-authority research system surrounding it.

This is not a dismissal.

The locomotion substrate contains several genuinely valuable and difficult achievements:

- whole-body physical truth;
- robust constrained-space traversal;
- bounded local avoidance;
- player-agency-sensitive motion;
- hard-vs-comfort separation;
- temporal embodiment;
- post-World failure/progress observation;
- strong causal provenance;
- participant/research Twin methodology.

But most of the repository's apparent “intelligence depth” does **not** currently participate in live action selection.

The ordinary companion's highest-level live objective remains the legacy eight-slot relational target.

## 2. Original project question vs current question

The Initial Research Skeleton did not define the mission as “solve following”.

It defined:

> a competent partner present in the same situation as the player.

The experiential north star requires that unscripted play increasingly produces:

- reliance without babysitting;
- enough predictability to coordinate;
- meaningful initiative;
- low-cost correction / direction by the player.

The original provisional sequence was:

### Stage A — physical cooperation apparatus

Expected to create the smallest world where cooperation can fail visibly, potentially including:

- player;
- one companion;
- obstacles/navigation;
- simple threats/opponents or equivalent pressure;
- enough combat/movement affordance to create spacing/positioning problems;
- strong debug.

### Stage B — minimum useful local partner

Explicit question:

> “With no micromanagement, is this beginning to behave like someone I can play alongside?”

Candidate phenomena already included:

- staying usefully near;
- not obstructing;
- **basic threat response**;
- **safe engagement/disengagement**;
- **simple assistance**;
- recovery.

### Stage C onward

Only later:

- command semantics;
- tactical cooperation under pressure;
- few companions;
- higher cognition.

### Current factual scope

The current authored World contains only:

- player;
- companion;
- open field;
- central pillar;
- narrow doorway;
- head-on contact.

There is no threat/opponent actor, attack, defense, assistance target, readiness transition or command.

Therefore:

> the project has not yet exercised the original Stage B product question.

It has deeply developed the locomotion/cooperation part of the Stage A→B boundary.

---

## 3. Was that drift unjustified?

No.

The chronology contains legitimate causal reasons for the movement depth.

### S0 — physical apparatus

The first apparatus proved enough physical stability to expose companion failures rather than hide them.

This remains durable.

### S1 — relational endpoint failure

Owner doorway evidence showed that a locally sensible relationship endpoint was insufficient because:

- traversal could be infeasible;
- constrained space could be contested;
- progress could fail without awareness.

The move into navigation/local locomotion was evidence-driven.

### S3 — spatial competence

S3 repaired a real missing layer:

- route became guidance;
- local motion became omnidirectional;
- whole-body feasibility and short-horizon player interaction entered movement selection.

Pillar and doorway competence were real bounded gains.

### S4 — temporal embodiment

S4 addressed another real Owner-visible problem: responsive decisions still looked digital.

Temporal continuity improved body realization without immediately losing S3 competence.

### R1 / Foundation — survival and causal truth

Owner browser evidence later falsified earlier mechanical confidence through an ordinary constrained-state shutdown.

Robustness work was not optional polish. It repaired:

- clearance-prison semantics;
- initial-overlap egress;
- zero-safe-velocity states;
- final-command hard safety;
- progress/recovery;
- runtime fault containment.

### CCC / A0 — explanation before wider authority

The first aliveness signal revealed a legitimate scientific problem:

> which apparently alive behavior comes from useful temporal embodiment and which comes from accidental bugs rendered smoothly?

CCC/A0 instrumentation and provenance were justified responses.

### Where the asymmetry begins

A1/A1.2 then expanded far beyond the size of the live brain while remaining intentionally zero/pass-through authority.

This work was disciplined and repeatedly falsified its own promotion shortcuts.

However:

> after the project had already established enough causal tooling to detect many important failures, additional decision-research depth grew much faster than the breadth of embodied teammate situations.

This is the first credible point of **research-apparatus overextension**.

Not because the work is invalid.

Because the project still had not earned a broader live decision problem than player-relative locomotion.

# Part II — True Live Runtime Anatomy

## 5. Actual ordinary authority chain

For `SPATIAL / NATURAL / A1 OFF`, the live action path is:

```text
same-step Owner movement
        ↓
canonical relationship orientation evidence
        ↓
legacy RelationalPositioningBrain
  eight heading-relative point slots
        ↓
one selected world target
        ↓
static hard-body route / lookahead
        ↓
S3/R1 local velocity candidates
  route + target + clearance + player-risk + continuity
        ↓
hard-vs-comfort candidate repair
        ↓
weighted local preferred-velocity refinement
        ↓
NATURAL acceleration/braking/jerk continuity
        ↓
final static hard-command constraint
        ↓
MotionIntent
        ↓
World / Rapier
        ↓
post-World progress/recovery classification
        ↓
at most: bounded local movement-state reset
```

CCC shadow and A1 are not inside this authority chain as decision owners.

## 6. Layer-by-layer classification

### 6.1 World / Rapier

**Class:** LIVE FACTUAL AUTHORITY  
**Durability:** HIGH

Owns:

- body state;
- movement legality/outcomes;
- collisions;
- static queries;
- authoritative tick progression.

This separation is one of the strongest durable project results.

No retrospective evidence currently argues for replacing it.

---

### 6.2 Canonical relationship orientation

**Class:** LIVE SEMANTIC INPUT  
**Durability:** MEDIUM/HIGH AS A SEAM

It distinguishes current Owner-directed movement meaning from body motion and ages semantic memory.

This repaired genuine provenance contamination.

However it expresses only a very narrow semantic fact:

> what recent direction, if any, is meaningfully attributable to Owner movement.

It is not situational awareness, tactical focus or teammate intention.

---

### 6.3 Legacy eight-slot relationship objective

**Class:** LIVE HIGHEST-LEVEL OBJECTIVE AUTHORITY  
**Durability:** LOW  
**Known pathologies:** HIGH

This remains the dominant semantic source of the ordinary companion's purpose.

Properties:

- eight heading-relative points;
- tactical reconsideration every 6 ticks;
- fixed preferred radius;
- score = front penalty + travel cost + switch cost + invalidity;
- deterministic lexical tie-breaking;
- remembers a previous semantic frame;
- when canonical orientation becomes NONE, can retain `RETAINED_LAST_SEMANTIC_FRAME`.

Qualified problems:

- PH-01: same symbolic slot can move the world target ~2.27 m on a 90° turn;
- PH-01: label can change while world target remains identical;
- fake side preference can originate in lexical tie order;
- PH-02: stale retained frame can materially prolong contact after semantic expiry;
- recovery objective identity is coupled to symbolic slot identity.

This module was explicitly called temporary in the Foundation authority map.

Retrospective judgement:

> it is a useful historical probe that became long-lived live authority because downstream competence improved faster than upstream relationship semantics.

It should receive **no architectural presumption** merely because many later systems consume it.

---

### 6.4 Static router

**Class:** LIVE MOVEMENT-COMPETENCE AUTHORITY  
**Durability:** HIGH FOR THE SEAM, MEDIUM/LOW FOR CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

Strong concepts:

- whole-body route feasibility;
- hard legality distinct from comfort clearance;
- egress-aware start semantics;
- route as guidance rather than motor command.

Known limitation:

- deterministic `pathKey` lexical ordering resolves symmetric equal-cost topology;
- PH-07 proved this leaks into embodied side choice with ~0.44 m mirror divergence.

The router has no semantic understanding of player cooperation or preference.

Retrospective judgement:

> keep the routing responsibility; do not confuse deterministic graph order with teammate intent.

---

### 6.5 S3 local spatial candidate scorer

**Class:** LIVE LOCAL MOTOR DECISION AUTHORITY  
**Durability:** MEDIUM

Current representation includes:

- 24 headings;
- fixed speed fractions plus adaptive near-goal sample;
- 0.55 s prediction horizon;
- static whole-body feasibility;
- route-lookahead cost;
- relationship-target distance;
- static-clearance quality;
- predicted player-clearance risk;
- previous-move continuity;
- unnecessary-motion penalty near target.

Current score remains a hand-authored scalar combination.

It can produce:

- ADVANCE;
- SIDESTEP;
- BACKOFF;
- HOLD.

But these labels are geometric/motor classifications.

The scorer has no representation of:

- threat;
- assistance;
- role;
- responsibility;
- engagement;
- readiness;
- command meaning;
- action identity;
- tactical objective beyond reaching/maintaining the relationship target.

Player cooperation here is mostly:

> do not predictively collide / pay extra cost near player motion.

PH-04 proves that this simple geometry can produce excellent open-space passing.

That should be preserved as evidence for **simplicity**, not reinterpreted as proof of social reasoning.

---

### 6.6 Hard-vs-comfort repair

**Class:** LIVE SAFETY/ROBUSTNESS AUTHORITY  
**Durability:** HIGH AS A PRINCIPLE

Strong durable concept:

- hard physical legality is not desired comfort;
- if already hard-overlapping, STOP is not a fake safe answer;
- explicit outward egress can be allowed;
- if no safe velocity exists, fail-close instead of throwing.

This layer mostly solves body competence rather than teammate cognition.

That is appropriate.

---

### 6.7 Preferred-velocity refinement

**Class:** LIVE MOTOR QUALITY HEURISTIC  
**Durability:** MEDIUM/LOW

It blends nearby near-best admissible velocity candidates and revalidates static/player clearance.

Useful effect:

- reduces coarse 24-direction quantization;
- creates more natural local motion.

Risk:

- another layer that can soften visible evidence of an upstream semantic discontinuity.

No evidence says the exact score window, temperature or contributor limit are durable.

---

### 6.8 NATURAL motion continuity

**Class:** LIVE TEMPORAL BODY AUTHORITY  
**Durability:** HIGH AS A PRINCIPLE, LOW/MEDIUM FOR CURRENT CONSTANTS

Uses actual velocity plus persistent acceleration to bound:

- acceleration;
- braking;
- steering;
- jerk.

This is one of the strongest candidates for a genuine source of embodiment/aliveness.

It provides real temporal state and makes the body behave like a continuing physical process.

But PH-01/PH-08 prove the central danger:

> temporal body coherence can visually counterfeit semantic intention coherence.

Retrospective rule:

**preserve embodiment, distrust the interpretation it creates.**

---

### 6.9 Final hard-command constraint

**Class:** LIVE LAST-MOMENT STATIC SAFETY  
**Durability:** HIGH FOR THE BOUNDARY

It prevents temporal shaping from invalidating already-proven static hard feasibility.

Important limitation:

- it is not a general social/player right-of-way authority.

That distinction should remain explicit.

---

### 6.10 Progress / recovery monitor

**Class:** LIVE POST-WORLD DIAGNOSIS + VERY NARROW LOCAL AUTHORITY  
**Durability:** HIGH AS EVIDENCE, LOW/MEDIUM AS CURRENT CONTROL

It can classify:

- ARRIVED;
- PROGRESSING;
- TRACKING_MOVING_OBJECTIVE;
- INTENTIONAL_HOLD;
- BLOCKED_PLAYER;
- BLOCKED_STATIC;
- NO_PROGRESS;
- ROUTE_INVALID;
- TRANSIENT/PERSISTENT_UNREACHABLE;
- RECOVERING.

It can emit actions named:

- WAIT_CONFLICT;
- RETRY_LOCAL;
- RECONSIDER_OBJECTIVE;
- REPORT_UNREACHABLE.

But the actual live supervisor only executes:

> **RETRY_LOCAL → reset local movement realization state.**

The source code explicitly states:

> higher-level actions such as RECONSIDER_OBJECTIVE are exposed but never executed here.

Therefore a crucial distinction is:

> the system often **knows how to name why its current plan is failing**, but does not yet have a higher-level live brain that acts on that diagnosis by forming a better plan.

This may be the single clearest technical boundary between the current substrate and a richer teammate.

---

### 6.11 CCC-0

**Class:** PASSIVE RESEARCH SHADOW  
**Live authority:** ZERO

Runs after the authoritative movement decision.

Can add research wall-clock cost.

Cannot choose the ordinary companion move.

The first Owner aliveness signal therefore cannot be credited to CCC cognition.

---

### 6.12 A1 / A1.2

**Class:** LARGE RESEARCH/COUNTERFACTUAL SYSTEM  
**Ordinary live authority:** ZERO

`A1AuthorityRuntime.resolveCompanionIntent()` deliberately returns the baseline move unchanged.

A1 provides valuable research capabilities:

- richer relative relationship representation;
- alternative player futures;
- same-physics rehearsals;
- candidate commands;
- safety;
- player-agency evidence;
- q/PACE/G4;
- first-divergence;
- robustness/metamorphic tests.

These are real capabilities of the **laboratory**.

They are not ordinary capabilities of the **companion**.

P2 is a separate explicit manual one-step intervention apparatus and does not change that statement.

---

### 6.13 Causal/debug system

**Class:** RESEARCH OBSERVABILITY

Exceptionally valuable for:

- causal reconstruction;
- provenance;
- Twin comparison;
- Owner incident capture.

But:

> a behavior being explainable to Browser GPT through a debug panel does not make that behavior readable to the player.

Debug maturity must not substitute for behavioral legibility.

## 7. What the live companion can fairly be credited with today

Within the tested movement domain, the ordinary companion can credibly be said to have:

- a persistent player-relative movement relationship;
- physically grounded body/contact;
- static obstacle traversal competence;
- local omnidirectional movement choice;
- short-horizon player-motion avoidance;
- bounded comfort-vs-hard-space behavior;
- temporally continuous physical motion;
- limited local history;
- basic progress/failure classification;
- bounded local recovery;
- autonomous reacquisition in some multi-beat movement episodes.

This is materially more than “follow the player”.

---

## 8. What it cannot fairly be credited with

The current ordinary live companion has not demonstrated:

- threat perception/assessment;
- tactical focus;
- engagement/disengagement judgement;
- helping the player with a world problem;
- selecting responsibility for a tactical situation;
- preserving an explicit action/intention object across beats;
- pre-combat readiness;
- attack/defense competence;
- role reasoning;
- command interpretation;
- autonomy-vs-obedience decisions;
- meaningful initiative beyond locomotion;
- explicit player correction/corrigibility;
- local goals unrelated to maintaining/recovering player-relative position;
- useful higher-cognition integration;
- few-companion cooperation.

Most importantly:

> no current experiment tests whether the companion can share responsibility for anything the player cares about **other than the pair's movement relationship itself**.

## 9. North-star continuity verdict

### What remained aligned

The project consistently protected several important north-star prerequisites:

- player agency;
- world-grounded causality;
- inspectability;
- physical competence;
- low-babysitting movement;
- robustness;
- temporal embodiment.

The long movement campaign was not arbitrary.

Owner failures repeatedly justified deeper work.

### What narrowed silently

The **object of cooperation** narrowed.

Original cooperation meant potentially:

- move together;
- respond to threats;
- assist;
- prepare;
- engage/disengage;
- obey/interpret direction;
- share tactical responsibility.

Current cooperation mostly means:

- maintain a player-relative relationship;
- avoid obstructing;
- traverse around the player/world;
- recover/reacquire.

That narrowing became easy to miss because the research apparatus around movement became extremely sophisticated.

### Current retrospective correction

Do not describe the next Owner experiment as:

> “test whether the accumulated Companion system feels like a teammate”

unless we explicitly intend that as a **very narrow locomotion-teammate question**.

A broader teammate claim has not been earned.
