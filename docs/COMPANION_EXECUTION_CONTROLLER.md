# Companion Brain Lab — Execution Controller

Status: **ACTIVE PROCESS AUTHORITY · QUESTION-DRIVEN**
Date: **2026-09-20**

Purpose:

> prevent a locally successful experiment from silently becoming the project's roadmap.

This document does not choose the final brain architecture, combat system, command grammar or next permanent feature. It defines how those choices may earn authority.

---

## 1. The failure pattern we are correcting

The project repeatedly produced a good retrospective and then lost its meaning during implementation.

Recurring sequence:

1. recover the real Owner problem;
2. choose one plausible local mechanism;
3. implement it;
4. machine-qualify it;
5. let code/tests/naming create psychological architecture authority;
6. continue deepening that mechanism because it now exists;
7. present an Owner build whose visible product delta is much smaller than the amount of work around it.

Observed examples include:

- research apparatus becoming much richer than ordinary live authority;
- movement review being presented after movement was already Owner-accepted;
- Stage B reducing shared responsibility to an advancing marker/intercept loop;
- the post-FAIL command/autonomy spike rapidly turning provisional F1/F2/F3 and numeric envelopes into an implied roadmap.

The correction is not “plan less” or “code less”.

The correction is:

> **design the experiment around the Owner question first, then allow mechanisms to earn their way into it.**

## 2. Keep five kinds of truth separate

### 2.1 Intent Ledger — normative authority

Records what the Owner actually wants, rejects, accepts or deliberately leaves open.

Examples:

- target = embodied teammate, not follower/pathfinding demo;
- Foundation movement = PASS · REWORK-READY;
- causal/debug observability is first-class apparatus;
- combat and pre-combat are important long-term domains;
- fast correction/direction matters;
- exact command grammar is open.

A mechanism cannot alter this ledger merely by existing.

### 2.2 Runtime Truth Ledger — descriptive authority

Records what moving `main` and qualified historical builds actually do.

Examples:

- authoritative domain currently has player + companion only;
- ordinary World command surface is movement-only;
- Stage B threat is a side-loop proxy;
- the repo contains a command/autonomy spike and a full causal workbench.

This ledger answers **what is**, never **what should be next**.

### 2.3 Hypothesis Ledger — prospective ideas

Candidate representations/mechanisms live here until promoted.

Examples:

- shared-danger encounter may be the best first teammate situation;
- player directives may be best represented as constraints;
- an embodied third entity may be the smallest useful world extension;
- pre-contact readiness may carry more teammate signal than attack choice.

Hypotheses are cheap to create and cheap to kill.

### 2.4 Evidence Ledger — observations

Evidence supports or falsifies bounded claims.

Keep evidence class explicit:

1. mechanical/contract;
2. controlled scenario;
3. runtime/visual observation;
4. Owner judgement.

A higher quantity of class-1 evidence cannot substitute for a missing class-4 judgement when the claim concerns feel/trust/readability.

### 2.5 Promotion Ledger — design decisions

A hypothesis may become active direction only through an explicit promotion decision.

Every promotion states:

- question being closed;
- evidence used;
- exact claim earned;
- claim limit / what remains unproven;
- falsifier that would reopen it;
- concrete authority granted by the promotion.

No promotion is implied by:
- code existing;
- tests passing;
- workflow naming;
- document length;
- time already invested;
- branch/commit count.

## 3. Promotion ladder

Use these statuses consistently:

`OPEN → HYPOTHESIS → SPIKE → MACHINE-QUALIFIED → OWNER-QUALIFIED / REJECTED → CLOSED-ENOUGH`

### OPEN
Known problem, representation intentionally unchosen.

### HYPOTHESIS
A proposed answer worth investigating.

### SPIKE
Implemented only to expose evidence. No product/roadmap authority.

### MACHINE-QUALIFIED
The mechanism does what its bounded contract says in controlled evidence.

This status does **not** mean it is useful, legible, fun or worth preserving.

### OWNER-QUALIFIED
The Owner has judged the relevant experiential question and the mechanism/situation survived that gate.

### REJECTED
Evidence or Owner judgement invalidated the product claim. Code may remain as donor/regression apparatus.

### CLOSED-ENOUGH
The decision is good enough to stop consuming attention until richer gameplay materially falsifies it.

Foundation movement is an example.

## 4. Two nested work loops

### Strategic loop — slow

Question:

> **What player-visible teammate uncertainty matters most now?**

Steps:

1. recover latest Owner intent/feedback;
2. inspect runtime truth;
3. identify the biggest missing teammate capability;
4. propose competing situations, not competing algorithms;
5. compare them for information gain, Owner-visible delta, reversibility and scope;
6. select one situation contract;
7. define claim budget + falsifiers;
8. only then authorize an execution loop.

Changing the strategic question requires an explicit promotion/replan.

### Execution loop — fast

Question:

> **What is the smallest reversible experiment that can close/falsify the current situation question?**

Steps:

1. reuse existing donors only where the specimen needs them;
2. make the smallest write;
3. run targeted mechanical/runtime evidence;
4. inspect visible behavior, not only assertions;
5. update evidence;
6. continue within the same claim budget or stop/replan if falsified.

A green execution loop never automatically advances the strategic loop.

## 5. Claim budget

Every experiment must declare before implementation:

### It may claim
The narrow behavioral/system fact it can actually establish.

### It may not claim
Tempting adjacent interpretations that are not tested.

Example:

A manual shared-danger apparatus might establish:

> player and companion can materially affect one authoritative world problem.

It may **not** establish:

> the companion has useful threat cognition.

A command arbitration spike may establish:

> directive and autonomous proposal can coexist and be inspected.

It may **not** establish:

> FOLLOW_ME is the right command or 3.5 m is the right leash.

## 6. Situation-first design rule

Before adding teammate cognition, make the situation itself worth solving.

Preferred order:

`Owner question → situation → world affordances/consequences → manual/simple baseline → missing cognition → integrated behavior`

Why:

- it prevents brain architecture from defining the problem it is supposed to solve;
- it distinguishes a bad AI from a bad scenario;
- it gives a baseline for “what useful cooperation could physically mean”;
- it lets the same situation compare manual/scripted/autonomous behavior;
- it avoids another side-loop marker whose only purpose is to trigger a selected algorithm.

### Manual/simple baseline rule

For a new shared-world situation, first prove that:

- the player can materially participate;
- the companion can materially participate under manual/trivial control;
- outcomes are World-owned;
- participation can help, interfere or fail;
- the situation remains readable without AI labels.

Only then give the local brain authority.

This baseline is apparatus qualification, not an Owner gameplay gate.

## 7. Next campaign — first situational teammate specimen

Current leading hypothesis:

> a compact shared-danger situation with a meaningful pre-contact window is the highest-information first teammate domain.

It remains a hypothesis until its apparatus contract is closed.

### Gate S0 — situation decision

Compare at least:
- shared danger / pre-contact;
- non-combat shared task;
- command-only cooperation;
- further Stage B refinement.

Promote a situation only if it:

1. cannot be convincingly faked by the accepted movement organism;
2. requires attention/responsibility beyond “stay near player”;
3. lets both player and companion materially affect one factual outcome;
4. supports useful initiative and possible interference;
5. makes low-cost correction/direction meaningful;
6. has a multi-beat before/during/after story;
7. can be built without broad game/framework scope.

Current evidence makes shared danger the leading candidate; this gate remains explicitly falsifiable.

### Gate S1 — apparatus-only world contract

No new autonomous teammate brain yet.

Design the smallest authoritative domain extension needed to express the situation.

Required properties:

- material problem exists in the same World progression;
- player and companion can both attempt relevant actions;
- action attempt is separate from execution/result;
- World determines legality and factual outcome;
- scenario can end in materially different consequences;
- causal/debug data exposes attempts and outcomes;
- no HP/inventory/combat framework unless the situation proves it necessary.

Use manual/trivial companion control to validate the apparatus.

**Promotion claim:** the world can host a real shared teammate problem.

### Gate S2 — situated attention / public responsibility

Give the local brain the smallest representation needed to answer:

> what currently deserves my attention, and is this problem mine to help with?

Do not start from a full behavior tree/utility/GOAP architecture.

Required evidence:

- problem can be noticed/ignored for a reason;
- public focus/responsibility identity exists;
- reason-for-change is inspectable;
- player remains a first-class situated reference, not merely another target.

**Promotion claim:** the companion can form a bounded situational responsibility.

### Gate S3 — first material autonomous contribution

Allow exactly enough live authority for the companion to materially change the shared situation.

Required qualities:

- action has a public identity;
- action uses existing body/movement competence as execution substrate;
- effect requires a real World attempt/outcome;
- initiative can help **or** interfere;
- completion/abandon/disengage condition is explicit;
- no success from proximity/timer alone unless that is physically the intended action.

**Promotion claim:** the companion can do one useful thing about a shared problem without micromanagement.

### Gate S4 — corrigibility / autonomy tension

Only after S3 creates something worth correcting.

Use the post-FAIL directive/arbitration spike as a donor, not an answer.

Research question:

> how can the Owner redirect/correct this real autonomous behavior cheaply without reducing the companion to a puppet?

Possible experiments may include constraints/priorities/targets/permissions, but exact command grammar stays open.

Required evidence:

- player direction can change behavior quickly;
- losing autonomous proposal remains inspectable;
- local discretion survives where compatible;
- unsafe/stupid literal obedience is falsifiable;
- command spam is not required.

**Promotion claim:** the first autonomous contribution is practically corrigible.

### Gate S5 — multi-beat continuity

Exercise:

`calm → awareness → readiness/preparation → contribution → changing situation → disengage/recovery → regroup`

Required evidence:

- upstream public action/state changes are coherent;
- NATURAL smoothing is not hiding decision thrash;
- stale intent/responsibility does not survive after its cause disappears;
- companion can return to ordinary relationship without ritual/reset.

**Promotion claim:** behavior forms an episode, not isolated reactive beats.

### Gate S6 — agent-side product falsification

Before Owner attention:

- run targeted contract tests;
- run browser/runtime specimen;
- inspect video/frames participant-first;
- deliberately try the explanation “same follower + new labels”;
- compare against Foundation baseline where meaningful;
- open causal microscope only after blind read;
- capture one or more failing incidents, not only success.

Do not proceed if the visible novelty is mostly instrumentation.

### Gate S7 — integrated Owner test

Only now spend Owner attention.

The build should contain in the **same runtime**:

- ordinary movement competence;
- material shared situation;
- autonomous responsibility/contribution;
- quick correction/direction if S4 was required;
- full causal workbench;
- Save/incident capture.

Owner question:

> does this begin to feel like a teammate sharing the situation with me rather than a follower executing another target?

Owner FAIL may invalidate:
- the situation;
- the responsibility representation;
- the action vocabulary;
- the correction model;
- the causal assumptions.

Do not default to weight/constant tuning.

## 8. Anti-regression triggers

Immediately stop and re-enter the strategic loop if:

1. the next step is justified mainly by “we already built X”;
2. a workflow/test name starts acting like roadmap authority;
3. more than one or two new systems appear before the current Owner question is sharper;
4. the experiment needs new nouns faster than it creates new player-visible verbs/consequences;
5. debug becomes richer while ordinary live behavior stays almost unchanged;
6. a marker/timer substitutes for a material world interaction;
7. the companion looks smarter only after reading labels;
8. parameter tuning begins before representation is validated;
9. a new Owner build removes observability to appear cleaner;
10. machine green is described as a product milestone;
11. the implementation plan survives major new evidence unchanged;
12. a short `kontynuuj` is interpreted as “execute the next numbered feature” rather than “continue solving the current Owner problem”.

---

## 9. What short “kontynuuj” means under this controller

1. restore latest Intent / Runtime Truth / Hypothesis / Evidence / Promotion state;
2. identify the current active gate;
3. test whether the gate and situation still deserve authority;
4. perform the smallest reversible step that increases information or closes the gate;
5. do not silently promote anything;
6. continue within the gate while progress remains justified;
7. stop only when:
   - Owner judgement is uniquely necessary;
   - a real strategic fork requires Owner preference;
   - evidence falsifies the active situation and requires replanning.
