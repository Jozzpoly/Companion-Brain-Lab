## Why this clarification exists

The Foundation campaign was not performed to make the current architecture sacred. It was performed to remove uncertainty, hidden failure and recovery debt so that the next phase could make large changes safely and causally.

The correct post-foundation posture is therefore:

> **the current runtime is a defended regression baseline and evidence source, not an architecture preservation target.**

CCC may replace, split, merge or remove previously defended seams when doing so creates a cleaner or more capable experiment. A change does not need to prove that the old seam is defective before it may be explored. The required discipline is different: preserve the ability to compare, explain and regress the consequences of the change.

## What Foundation PASS actually bought us

Foundation PASS gives the project:

- a known-good checkpoint to return to;
- causal instrumentation that makes regressions easier to localize;
- defended failure semantics for overlap, egress, route loss, recovery and catastrophic faults;
- reproducible fixtures and automated gates;
- confidence that aggressive redesign is not being performed on top of unknown substrate breakage.

It does **not** mean that World boundaries, routing contracts, movement contracts, temporal realization, scene orchestration, debug structure or any other current implementation must survive CCC.

Rapier, fixed-step World progression, the current router, the local velocity lattice, progress/recovery and final guards are all donors/current-best mechanisms. They may remain because they continue to earn their place, not because Foundation PASS granted them permanent authority.

## Rework rule

During CCC, prefer the architecture that best serves the current research question and long-term teammate objective.

When changing a previously defended mechanism:

1. retain or recreate the relevant falsifiers;
2. compare against the known-good foundation behavior where the comparison remains meaningful;
3. keep causal evidence strong enough to explain gains and regressions;
4. delete obsolete compatibility structure instead of preserving it by inertia;
5. do not retain an old abstraction merely because tests already exist around it.

The burden is **evidence about consequences**, not justification for permission to experiment.

## Correction to the planning skeleton

Phrases such as `foundation-preserving`, `preserve by default` and `design invariant` in the earlier CCC planning material must be read narrowly:

- `foundation-preserving` means preserve the ability to detect substrate regressions, not preserve the present architecture;
- `preserve by default` means a useful donor can remain until a better experiment replaces it, not that replacement requires extraordinary proof;
- the upstream-cooperation vs downstream-physical-guard split is a **strong evidence-backed working hypothesis**, not an untouchable invariant. It should be deliberately challenged if later evidence, intentional close-contact behavior, commands, combat or multi-companion coordination demand a different responsibility boundary.

## Why CCC-0 remains deliberately zero-authority

CCC-0 is intentionally conservative only in one dimension: its shadow calculations must not change authoritative movement.

That is an experimental control, not architectural caution.

The purpose is to create same-tick evidence for WHERE, PACE and PLAYER COOPERATION, expose disagreements with the legacy system, recover useful donor ideas and build falsifiers before authority promotion. Once that evidence is legible, subsequent CCC stages are free to replace large portions of the current coordination/movement stack rather than incrementally patching it.

## Long-term criterion

The target is not preservation of the current companion, current movement architecture or even the current CCC skeleton.

The target is a companion that increasingly behaves like a competent teammate: present in the world, readable, useful without babysitting, able to coordinate with the player, later able to accept fast commands, fight and cooperate with a small number of other companions.

If reaching that target requires breaking the current architecture, CCC is explicitly allowed to break it.
