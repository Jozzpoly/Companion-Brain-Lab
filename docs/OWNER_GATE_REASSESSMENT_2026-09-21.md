# Owner Gate Reassessment — 2026-09-21

Status: **OWNER EVIDENCE INVALIDATES S7 PROMOTION · PUBLIC SPECIMEN RETAINED AS DIAGNOSTIC**

## Trigger

The Owner interacted with the public `/teammate-readiness/?teammate=1` specimen and reported that `E` and `Q` appeared to do nothing. A ~20 second Owner recording was reviewed participant-first.

This is not dismissed as user error. It is direct product evidence against the gate design.

## What the recording shows

Participant-first, the experience remains dominated by three abstract bodies:

- blue player;
- yellow companion;
- orange hostile.

The player moves continuously while the hostile follows. The companion maintains/re-anchors a relative position, but the sequence does not communicate a robust shared episode with an intelligible causal arc.

At roughly the middle of the recording the HUD does show:

`Q HELD · companion intervention withheld`

so the raw Q input path was registered at least once.

That strengthens, rather than weakens, the negative conclusion:

> technically receiving Q is not the same as giving the Owner a meaningful corrective action.

No material world change made the correction legible.

The recording contains no participant-legible successful E intervention.

## Why E can honestly feel dead

The current S1 action contract permits `INTERVENE` to succeed only when:

1. shared danger is already in hidden phase `WINDUP`; and
2. the acting body is within 0.9 m of the hostile.

The windup lasts 45 World ticks at 60 Hz:

`45 / 60 = 0.75 seconds`.

Any E press outside that hidden 0.75 s phase is `INVALID_PHASE`; any press farther than 0.9 m is `OUT_OF_RANGE`.

The participant HUD deliberately hides phase and action-outcome explanations.

Therefore an ordinary human can press the advertised E control repeatedly and receive no readable response.

This is apparatus behavior, not acceptable gameplay affordance design.

## Why Q can honestly feel dead

Q does not express a broad player intention such as retreat, stay, cover me, hold position, do not engage, or leave this target.

It toggles only:

`WITHHOLD_CURRENT_CONTRIBUTION`

downstream of one S3 contribution.

Outside the short interval where that contribution would execute, Q can change internal state while producing no material world difference.

The automated smoke knew exactly when to hold Q because it polled hidden causal text for:

- `phase WINDUP`;
- `responsibility OWNED`;
- raw S3 proposal;
- `blocked YES`.

That is valid machine evidence for the S4 seam.

It is invalid evidence that Q is a usable Owner-facing correction.

## Process error

The project controller explicitly defined:

### S5 — multi-beat continuity

`calm → awareness → readiness/preparation → contribution → changing situation → disengage/recovery → regroup`

before:

### S6 — agent-side product falsification

and:

### S7 — integrated Owner test.

The readiness tranche was promoted directly to S7 without earning S5.

That promotion was wrong.

Static/browser artifacts also over-weighted selected causal frames. They were insufficient to establish the temporal legibility of the continuous participant experience.

The Owner recording is stronger evidence for that question.

## Higher-level diagnosis

The current public specimen combines several valid research seams:

- S1 World action/outcome authority;
- S2 situated responsibility;
- S3 bounded autonomous contribution;
- S4 downstream correction;
- pre-contact readiness.

But it still composes them inside an **apparatus**, not a sufficiently game-like cooperative episode.

The apparatus has these structural problems as an Owner test:

1. the hostile is still an abstract sensor/direct-motion stimulus;
2. the player can keep moving/kiting without entering a meaningful interaction;
3. readiness can read as a geometric formation offset rather than situated understanding;
4. intervention is a state-validity event, not a materially readable embodied action;
5. E has a hidden 0.75 s validity window and silent failure;
6. Q is a mechanism-specific veto with little world-visible effect;
7. there is no genuine multi-beat continuity/regroup loop;
8. the player is being asked to test mechanism contracts rather than naturally play;
9. the causal workbench can explain the mechanism, but the world itself still does not explain enough of the behavior.

## Revised meaning of Owner test

A serious Owner test in Companion Brain Lab must be a **small playable cooperative episode**, not a mechanism proof with participant controls.

It does not need final art or final combat architecture.

It does need enough world truth and continuity that the Owner can play naturally and encounter the thesis of the project without knowing hidden state.

Minimum requirements:

- a legible material problem with stakes/consequence;
- a player action that has obvious affordance and immediate readable feedback;
- companion autonomy that matters without debug ceremony;
- preparation before action;
- player movement/intent remains first-class;
- a correction/redirection that expresses a useful player intention and visibly changes behavior;
- engagement or assistance that changes the same material world;
- disengagement/recovery;
- return/regroup into the ordinary player-companion relation;
- repeated or sustained play long enough to expose continuity, not one hidden timing window;
- full causal workbench and incident capture remain available for forensic explanation after the first impression.

A good Owner test should naturally support counterfactual play such as:

- player does nothing;
- player commits first;
- player backs away/disengages;
- player changes direction during preparation;
- player takes over the problem;
- player corrects companion initiative;
- situation ends and both actors must recover/regroup.

The Owner should not need to know a phase table to make those experiments meaningful.
