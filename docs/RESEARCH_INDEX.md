## Naming caution

Many files call themselves `PLAN`, `CURRENT`, `QUALIFICATION`, `PASS` or `OWNER`; those words describe a **dated local claim**. The repo's present authority is grounded in evidence and newer Owner feedback, not a file's title.

## Keep history without turning it into a museum

If you find an old mechanism truly unused, prove its dependencies/tests/Pages requirements first. Prefer a well-scoped deletion with reproducible tests over deleting wide areas because file names look stale. Leave a brief exact-source recovery pointer, and do not continually write duplicate summaries.
