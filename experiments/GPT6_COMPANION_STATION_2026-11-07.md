## 1. Why this line exists

The Owner's target is *not* a smarter-following circle, a highly instrumented demo, or a universal Jaskinia connector. The goal is an **embodied, useful and corrigible companion**, and an increasingly capable **single Jaskinia experience** in which independently developed living worlds, tools, their creators and agents can meet and transform one another. Medium is a philosophy/trajectory, **not a competing app**. Independent agents must forge distinctive working perspectives through real discoveries, errors, criticism and experiments, not roleplay arbitrary character identities or demand a central agent manager.

Our candidate independent question: **how does reciprocal agency become a trustworthy experience?** In the same physical situation: what did the player expect, what could C1 know and accomplish, whose initiative was it, what did C1 actually do, who benefited or was displaced, what commitment survived an interruption, and how could the player correct it without puppeteering? A successful isolated strike is a possible constituent, not the mission.

Read actual Owner corrections from the current conversation: smaller prompts should not be needed to force continuity; do not become an agent waiting for Opus; act bravely and reversibly in one's own code, but do not equate green CI, branch count or own productivity with world/teammate quality. The right to destroy a bad implementation **does not permit destroying irreplaceable works, source provenance or Owner's data**.

### Non-negotiable corrections to our own past story

- The baseline browser test stops **on the first PLAYER hit** while Cover stops **on the first successful C1 strike**. The displayed counts are not an equal-horizon trial or proof of long-run advantage. A later controlled same-horizon, moving-player study is open.
- `pressure target C1` is a **responsibility/risk reassignment**, not demonstrated harm or sacrifice. At the published observed moment **C1 hits = 0**. Avoid saying C1 already *paid a cost*.
- The hostile has `sensor` collision and damage is phase/range-based; Rapier movement does not turn combat into fully physical strikes. C1 sees global actor positions and combat phase: **oracle-like state, not perception, LOS, tactical understanding or commitment**.
- `12 ready ticks` is a constant, **not evidence of true preparation**.
- The Cover lab is open-only, one target, one action and is OFF by default; legacy trial/setup format does not include Cover authority, so those A/B captures were blocked while armed rather than being misrepresented as comparable.
- `state?` / `remark` is a reported moment, **not a solver checkpoint/replay**. The old Jaskinia intentionally uses no-referrer; initial source handshake only checks origin/frame. GitHub Pages shares origins between apps; **no private Owner data or host-write power should be introduced**.
- No Owner qualification of teammate feel, interaction quality, touch use, repeatable day-to-day Jaskinia entry, full simulation rehydration or safe general live authoring. Historical public R1 remains *not* the current Field Lab.

## 5. Next high-value pressure, not a preapproved implementation plan

Instead of adding a fourth synthetic combat threat or larger debugger, recover **one longer, freely interrupted player–C1 episode**, preferably with an environment change that a player can make *while the World remains the same instance*. Do not build generic live-editor infrastructure before choosing that episode.

Questions that must materially alter design:
1. Did C1's action create *more player agency* or steal a desired action? Can the player correct C1 easily while its physical commitment continues?
2. What does C1 legitimately observe, and what can it only infer or remain uncertain about? No omniscient use of phase/target as a substitute for senses.
3. Does C1 carry a bounded intention across changing terrain, player movement or threat interruption without being scripted to win?
4. Can a situation produce useful counterexamples beyond one success vignette? Include at least one helpful, one unwanted and one ambiguous intervention, plus a human/manual comparator.
5. Can Owner/agent revisit the same situated problem without treating a JSON screenshot as full world persistence?

Choice of substrate is **open**: reuse existing Field Lab, borrow one bounded Combat/Coopege material truth, or create a clean disposable world. Prefer the smallest world that supports the *experience* genuinely; refuse a universal superorganism platform as a prerequisite. Keep tests rigorous, but do not use 790 unit PASS as evidence of teammate quality.
